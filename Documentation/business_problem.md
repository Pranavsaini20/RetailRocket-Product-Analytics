# Business Problem and Objectives

## Context
E-commerce platforms record visitor interactions such as product views, add-to-cart events and transactions. Analyzing these events can help identify engagement patterns and potential friction in the purchase journey.

## Problem statement
Analyze RetailRocket behavioral data and turn it into business-oriented insights about user activity, product performance, category engagement and conversion behavior.

## Objectives
1. Measure user activity and event distribution.
2. Examine product and category engagement.
3. Compare views, add-to-cart actions and transaction events.
4. Define KPIs for executive reporting.
5. Present findings in Power BI.
6. Recommend areas for further investigation.

## Analytical questions
- How many distinct visitors and events are represented?
- Which event types and products receive the most activity?
- How do views, carts and transactions compare?
- What does the current cart-abandonment proxy indicate?
- Which products/categories warrant further review?
- How can the findings support business decisions?

## Scope
Four Power BI pages: Executive Overview, User Analytics, Product Analytics and Executive Insights.

## Caution
Event counts are not automatically equivalent to unique users, sessions, carts, orders or revenue. Every metric must be interpreted using its defined numerator and denominator.
