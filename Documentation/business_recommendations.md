# Key Findings and Business Recommendations

## Findings from the current report
- Approximately 1.41M distinct visitors and 2.76M event rows are represented.
- The derived product-category table contains approximately 417K products.
- The current event-count conversion measure is approximately 0.84%.
- The current cart-abandonment proxy is approximately 67.61%.
- Category IDs 1051 and 1483 show comparatively high activity in the current report.
- High engagement does not automatically mean a product will convert.

## Recommendations
1. **Investigate funnel drop-off:** compare views, add-to-cart and transactions; label event-based ratios clearly.
2. **Review high-view/low-purchase products:** examine price, availability, product content, reviews and delivery promise where data is available.
3. **Compare availability with demand:** validate availability values and their time relevance before inventory decisions.
4. **Segment performance:** compare category, product and time periods rather than relying only on platform-wide averages.
5. **Refine metric definitions:** use consistent user/session/order grains and distinct transaction IDs when reliable.
6. **Extend the project:** add cohort retention, time-aware product properties, automated refresh and data-quality tests.

These are hypotheses and areas for investigation, not proven causal explanations. The dataset may not include all information needed to diagnose pricing, checkout, marketing or inventory causes.
